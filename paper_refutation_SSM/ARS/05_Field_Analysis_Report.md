## Review Strategy Recommendations
- **Key Characteristics**: The paper is highly technical but short. It relies on a mathematical proof that shows an algebraic contradiction. Thus, the review must balance a verification of the mathematics with a critique of the economic realism of the core assumptions (specifically the long-run stability of $\theta$).
- **Complementarity & Tension**:
  - **Tension**: There is a clear tension between Peer Reviewer 2 (Sraffian) and Peer Reviewer 3 (Post-Keynesian). Reviewer 2 will likely accept the assumption of a given distribution and a fixed $\theta$ for the sake of Sraffian logic, focusing on the choice of technique. Reviewer 3 will attack this assumption as a "specification tautology" that forces instability by assuming a long-run drift in the capital-capacity ratio.
  - **Complementarity**: Reviewer 1 (Methodology) and the EIC will complement each other. Reviewer 1 will verify if the math is sound enough to merit publication, while the EIC will determine if the debate is of sufficient interest to the journal's readers.
- **Synthesis Action**: The editor (or the synthesizer) must ensure that the author addresses Reviewer 3's conceptual critique (perhaps by adding a discussion of how induced technical change or endogenous distribution might force $\theta \to 1$ in the long run) without letting it overshadow the valid mathematical proof verified by Reviewer 1.
