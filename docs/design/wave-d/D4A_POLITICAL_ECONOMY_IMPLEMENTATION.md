## 2. Historical Source-Criticism Protocol Implementation

Implemented in [`shared/protocols/historical_source_criticism_protocol.md`](file:///c:/ReposGitHub/academic-research-skills/shared/protocols/historical_source_criticism_protocol.md):
- **Claim-Relative Evaluation (§1, §5):** Evaluates every historical source strictly as a `(source, claim)` pair typed across nine atomic `claim_type` categories (`OCCURRENCE`, `TIMING`, `ACTOR_BELIEF_OR_PERCEPTION`, `ACTOR_INTENTION`, `DECISION_PROCESS`, `INSTITUTIONAL_RULE`, `MAGNITUDE`, `ABSENCE`, `RELATION`).
- **No Universal Hierarchy (§4):** Explicitly prohibits `PRIMARY SOURCE > SECONDARY SOURCE` or `ARCHIVAL > PUBLISHED` rankings, demonstrating why a source can be `SUPPORTS (STRONG)` for one claim (`what the Treasury argued in October`) and `SUPPORTS (WEAK) [INTERESTED_PARTY]` for another (`why the Cabinet voted as it did`).
- **Canonical Diagnostic Repertoire (`D1`–`D14`) (§5.2):** Implemented under `CAPABILITY SUPPORT ≠ UNIVERSAL APPLICATION REQUIREMENT` (`D4-L3`): `D1`–`D14` constitute a diagnostic repertoire to draw upon where relevant. Only applicable dimensions need explicit treatment, preserving the mandatory core obligation (access, temporality, incentives, corroboration, provenance, silence). Covers Provenance & Custody (`D1`), Authenticity (`D2`), Authorship & Institutional Position (`D3`), Temporal Proximity (`D4`), Contemporaneous vs. Retrospective Construction (`D5`), Directness of Access (`D6`), Intended Audience & Genre (`D7`), Strategic Interests & Incentives (`D8`), Internal Consistency (`D9`), External Corroboration & Independence (`D10`), Archival Selection, Survival & Silence (`D11`), Numerical Construction Provenance (`D12`), Conceptual & Temporal Fit (`D13`), and Source Role in the Argument (`D14`).
- **Canonical Verdict Taxonomy (§6.1):** Structured according to the unified taxonomy: `SUPPORTS (STRONG | MODERATE | WEAK)`, `UNDERMINES (STRONG | MODERATE | WEAK)`, `SILENT` (with `subtype: DIAGNOSTIC` requiring verified `trace_expectation_basis` and `subtype: NON_DIAGNOSTIC` supplying zero evidence for absence), or `INDETERMINATE`, plus explicit risk flags (`SINGLE_SOURCE`, `INTERESTED_PARTY`, `ADMISSION_AGAINST_INTEREST`, `RETROSPECTIVE_RECONSTRUCTION`, `INDIRECT_ACCESS`, `DE_JURE_ONLY`, `INTERPOLATED_OR_SPLICED_SERIES`).
- **Historical Statistics Boundary (§7):** Enforces the strict separation between `CONSTRUCTION PROVENANCE` (who compiled the series, archival basis, coverage gaps, changing definitions, benchmark interpolation—owned by this protocol) and `STOCHASTIC ADMISSIBILITY` (integration order, cointegration, structural break significance, estimator validity—owned by `applied-econometrics-pe`).

```text
OBSERVED BEHAVIOR (D4-L1):
Generic research workflows frequently treat source quality as an intrinsic document-level
property (e.g., ranking primary archival documents above secondary monographs, or official
records above retrospective accounts, regardless of the proposition being tested).

ARCHITECT INTERVENTION:
Mandated that `shared/protocols/historical_source_criticism_protocol.md` evaluate evidence
strictly relative to the specific claim being supported, and prohibited encoding
`PRIMARY SOURCE > SECONDARY SOURCE` as a universal hierarchy.

LEARNED PRINCIPLE:
Evidentiary weight in historical and documentary inquiry is a function of the `(source, claim)`
pair (`CLAIM-RELATIVE EVIDENTIARY WARRANT ≠ INTRINSIC SOURCE HIERARCHY`). The same document
may be authoritative operational evidence for one atomic claim (e.g., what an actor stated
in a meeting) and biased, indirect, or non-diagnostic for another (e.g., why the institution
ultimately acted).

GENERALIZATION SCOPE:
CROSS-DOMAIN

PROMOTION STATUS:
PROTOCOL (`shared/protocols/historical_source_criticism_protocol.md`)

RATIONALE:
Any domain skill or review workflow that evaluates documentary, archival, legal, or
institutional evidence requires claim-relative assessment to avoid both naive primary-source
fetishism and false dismissal of retrospective or secondary evidence.
```

```text
OBSERVED BEHAVIOR (D4-L2):
Long-run historical datasets (reconstructed national accounts, spliced censuses, historical
wage/price series) sit at the boundary between historical source criticism and econometrics,
creating a risk that historians rule on time-series properties or econometricians overlook
benchmark interpolation artifacts.

ARCHITECT INTERVENTION:
Mandated an explicit boundary distinguishing `CONSTRUCTION PROVENANCE` (who constructed the

For historical quantitative series, `CONSTRUCTION PROVENANCE` belongs to historical source

econometrics (`CONSTRUCTION PROVENANCE ≠ STOCHASTIC ADMISSIBILITY`).

GENERALIZATION SCOPE:
CROSS-DOMAIN

PROMOTION STATUS:
PROTOCOL & LOCK (`historical_source_criticism_protocol.md` §7, `SKILL.md` §4.2)

RATIONALE:

that mechanical artifacts (such as linear interpolation between census years) are surfaced
before substantive or econometric conclusions are drawn.
```
