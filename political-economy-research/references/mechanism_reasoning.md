## 1. Canonical Five-Link Mechanism Grammar (`M-1`)

Every historical causal explanation evaluated by `political-economy-research` must decompose candidate explanations into an auditable chain of five linked components:

```text
ANTECEDENT
    Initial structural, institutional, or macro-historical conditions
        ↓
MECHANISM
    Causal trigger or generative force posited by the hypothesis
        ↓
INTERMEDIATE PROCESS
    Concrete transmission channel through which pressures or resources propagate
        ↓
ACTOR / INSTITUTIONAL RESPONSE
    Identifiable actors or institutions perceiving, contesting, deciding, or adapting
        ↓
CONSEQUENCE
    Resulting institutional transformation, policy shift, or political-economic outcome
```

> **FUNDAMENTAL RULE (`M-4` & `M-10`):** A temporal sequence ($A$ preceded $B$) or a macro correlation ($X$ covaries with $Y$) is **never** a mechanism unless the `INTERMEDIATE PROCESS` and `ACTOR / INSTITUTIONAL RESPONSE` links are specified and evidenced.

## 2. Non-Doctrinal Actor & Institutional Operationalization (`O5`)

### 2.1 Theory as Hypothesis Input, Never Doctrinal Canon
`political-economy-research` operationalizes theoretical frameworks across political-economy traditions into competing, empirically falsifiable mechanisms without privileging any school as default truth:
1. **Specify Actors & Institutional Arena:** Name the concrete actors (state ministries, central banks, industrial fractions, financial syndicates, trade unions, agrarian landlords, political parties, international creditors) and the institutional rules governing their interaction.
2. **State Theorized Interests & Constraints:** Articulate what each rival theory predicts the relevant actors wanted, feared, or were constrained from doing.
3. **Derive Divergent Observable Traces:** Identify where Mechanism $M_A$ and Rival Mechanism $M_B$ predict *different* chronological sequences, *different* initiating actors, *different* internal debate records, or *different* distributional winners. Where two theories predict identical traces, record that the available evidence cannot discriminate between them.

### 2.2 Illustrative Non-Canonical Mechanism Families (`NON-EXHAUSTIVE, NON-RANKED`)
Any subset of the following families (or user-supplied alternatives) may compete or combine in an inquiry:
- *Distributional & Class-Fraction Conflict:* Wage/profit/exchange-rate struggles among labor organizations, industrial capital, export elites, and financial creditors.
- *Elite Coalition & Sectoral Realignment:* Shifts in governing coalitions across tradable/non-tradable sectors, state enterprises, or regional blocs.
- *Bureaucratic Capacity & State Autonomy:* Administrative bottlenecks, fiscal extraction limits, or intra-state rivalry between planning ministries and central banks.
- *Institutional Evolution:* Incremental transformation via institutional layering, drift, conversion, displacement, or exhaustion.
- *External Balance, Debt & Monetary Constraint:* Foreign-exchange exhaustion, sovereign refinancing freezes, or international institutional conditionality.
- *Electoral & Party Competition:* Median-voter pressures, clientelist coalition maintenance, or partisan survival incentives.
- *Ideational & Technocratic Translation:* Adoption of new policy paradigms by insulated technocratic networks.

## 3. Mandatory Process-Tracing Reasoning Obligations (`M-1`–`M-11`)

Regardless of which terminology or formatting a project prefers, every process-tracing analysis must satisfy eleven core methodological obligations:

| ID | Obligation | Analytical Requirement |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **`M-1`** | **Step Decomposition** | Decompose each mechanism into `ANTECEDENT → MECHANISM → INTERMEDIATE PROCESS → ACTOR / INSTITUTIONAL RESPONSE → CONSEQUENCE`. |
| **`M-2`** | **Rival Mechanisms** | Formulate at least one substantive rival mechanism (plus a timing-coincidence or common-shock null where appropriate). Declaring "no plausible rival" requires explicit justification. |
| **`M-3`** | **Expected Observable Implications** | For each link in each rival mechanism, specify ex ante what documentary, institutional, behavioral, or quantitative traces should exist if that link operated. |
| **`M-4`** | **Sequence & Timing Evidence** | Verify whether antecedent conditions preceded intermediate processes and whether actor deliberations preceded the final decision/outcome. |
| **`M-5`** | **Disciplined Negative Evidence** | Treat the absence of an expected trace as evidence *against* a mechanism **only** when a positive `trace_expectation_basis` establishes that the step would have generated a record and that the record would have survived in the consulted archive (`historical_source_criticism_protocol.md` D11). |
| **`M-6`** | **Evidential Diagnosticity** | Evaluate how strongly an observed trace discriminates between rival mechanisms: is the evidence much more expected under $M_1$ than under $M_2$, or predicted equally by both? |
| **`M-7`** | **Within-Case vs. Cross-Case Separation** | Distinguish within-case process traces from cross-case comparative patterns (`M1`), stating clearly what each contributes. |
| **`M-8`** | **Equifinality & Conjunctural Causation** | Account for **equifinality** (different causal paths producing similar outcomes across cases) and **conjunctural causation** (where Mechanism $A$ produces the outcome only in combination with Condition $B$). Never force a monocausal narrative onto conjunctural evidence. |
| **`M-9`** | **Explicit Scope Conditions** | Bound all mechanism findings to the specific cases and historical periods (`M2`) examined. |
| **`M-10`** | **Mechanism Specificity** | Generic evidence that "economic turmoil existed" or "business was unhappy" does not substantiate a specific coalition-veto or capital-strike mechanism without trace evidence of the specific transmission channel. |
| **`M-11`** | **Causal-Process Observations (`CPOs`)** | Distinguish within-case **Causal-Process Observations (`CPOs`)**—diagnostic pieces of evidence about context, sequence, actor deliberation, or mechanism transmission—from rectangular **Dataset Observations (`DSOs`)**. |

## 4. Supported vs. Mandatory Process-Tracing Vocabularies (`CAPABILITY SUPPORT ≠ UNIVERSAL APPLICATION REQUIREMENT`)

In strict compliance with **`CAPABILITY SUPPORT ≠ UNIVERSAL APPLICATION REQUIREMENT`**:

### 4.1 Van Evera Diagnostic Test Labels (`OPTIONAL VOCABULARY — NOT MANDATORY SERIALIZATION`)
The classic four-part test typology—`straw-in-the-wind`, `hoop`, `smoking-gun`, and `doubly-decisive`—is **SUPPORTED as an optional heuristic vocabulary** for summarizing `M-6` qualitative diagnosticity:
- **`straw-in-the-wind` *(optional label)*:** Raises or lowers plausibility slightly; neither necessary nor sufficient for the mechanism.
- **`hoop` *(optional label)*:** Necessary for the mechanism to remain viable (failing the test substantially undermines the mechanism), but passing does not eliminate rivals that predict the same trace.
- **`smoking-gun` *(optional label)*:** High uniqueness/sufficiency for the mechanism step if the source is authentic, corroborated, and uncontradicted, though absence does not falsify the mechanism. *(Warning: A single apparent "smoking gun" contradicted by multiple independent records triggers `UNRESOLVED_CONTRADICTION` in `M4` and cannot establish strong attribution.)*
- **`doubly-decisive` *(optional label)*:** Both necessary for the proposed mechanism and incompatible with rivals.

> **BINDING NON-MANDATE:** Van Evera labels are **never mandatory serialization**. A project or manuscript that executes process tracing using direct qualitative diagnosticity (`HIGH_DISCRIMINATION`, `MODERATE_DISCRIMINATION`, `NON_DISCRIMINATING`) or narrative step-by-step evaluation without mentioning `straw-in-the-wind`, `hoop`, `smoking-gun`, or `doubly-decisive` is **100% compliant** with this skill.

### 4.2 Bayesian / Likelihood-Ratio Process Tracing (`OPTIONAL — PROJECT-DECLARED ONLY`)
- Qualitative likelihood reasoning (asking whether evidence $E$ is more plausible under $M_1$ than under $M_2$) is part of `M-6`.
- **No Mandatory Bayesian Quantification:** Do **not** require or invent numerical prior probabilities $P(M)$ or numerical likelihood ratios $P(E \mid M_1)/P(E \mid M_2)$ unless the user or project explicitly supplies a justified probabilistic research design.

### 4.3 Supported Artifact Schemas (`A3` & `A4` — `OPTIONAL SERIALIZATION`)
High-stakes adjudications, chapters, or formal evaluations may serialize mechanism specifications and evidence matrices using `A3` and `A4` below. Lightweight inquiries may present the same reasoning in structured analytical prose.

#### Schema `A3`: Mechanism Map (`OPTIONAL SERIALIZATION`)
- `mechanism_id` & `label`: Identifier and theoretical lineage (entered as hypothesis).
- `antecedent_conditions`: Initial structural/institutional conditions.
- `mechanism_trigger`: Generative causal force.
- `intermediate_process_steps[]`: Ordered sequence of transmission links, each specifying `actor_or_institution`, `action_or_deliberation`, `expected_trace`, and `expected_timing`.
- `actor_or_institutional_response`: Concrete decision or institutional adaptation.
- `downstream_consequence`: Target outcome explained.
- `scope_conditions`: Applicable cases and periods (`M2`).
- `implications_by_method`:
  - `historical_traces`: Documentary/archival expectations (`M3`);
  - `econometric_implications`: Testable parameter/break predictions exported to `applied-econometrics-pe`;
  - `formal_model_implications`: Closure or comparative-static links to `heterodox-macro-modeling`.

#### Schema `A4`: Claim–Evidence–Mechanism Matrix (`OPTIONAL SERIALIZATION`)
- `evidence_item_id` & `protocol_pair_verdict`: Link to `(source, claim)` assessment under `historical_source_criticism_protocol.md` (or econometric/model input).
- `atomic_claim_supported`: Specific step or timing claim.
- `predictions_across_rivals`: For each rival mechanism ($M_1, M_2, \dots$), record whether the evidence is `EXPECTED`, `NOT_EXPECTED`, or `NEUTRAL`.
- `diagnosticity`: Qualitative discriminating power across rivals (with optional Van Evera label if requested).
- `negative_evidence_audit`: If evaluating an absent trace, state the `trace_expectation_basis` (`DIAGNOSTIC_SILENCE` vs. `NON_DIAGNOSTIC_SILENCE`).
- `unresolved_contradictions` & `evidence_gaps`: Contradicting records and resulting **Evidence Requests** (`M1` §5).

## 5. Rotated Non-Canonical Illustrations (`ILLUSTRATION — NON-CANONICAL`)

1. **Illustration 1 (Strong Temporal Sequence vs. Missing Intermediate Mechanism — Interwar Gold Standard Exit):**
   - *Observation:* Industrial unemployment rises in Quarter 1; central bank suspends gold convertibility in Quarter 3.
   - *Rival Mechanisms:* $M_1$ (Trade-union parliamentary pressure forced suspension to relieve unemployment) vs. $M_2$ (Foreign short-term deposit runs exhausted gold reserves despite central bank preference to stay on gold).
   - *Process-Tracing Audit:* Chronological sequence ($Q_1 \to Q_3$) is consistent with both (`NON_DISCRIMINATING`). Treasury and central bank daily reserve ledgers plus cabinet minutes show ministers rejecting union appeals in $Q_2$ and suspending convertibility only on the afternoon foreign-exchange reserves fell below statutory cover (`M_2` corroborated at every intermediate link; $M_1$ lacks the intermediate response link despite temporal precedence).
2. **Illustration 2 (Single "Smoking-Gun" Document Contradicted by Independent Records — 1970s Industrial Subsidy Reform):**
   - *Observation:* A private letter from an industrial association president claims his lobbying single-handedly wrote the 1974 export-subsidy decree.
   - *Process-Tracing Audit:* Although the letter looks like a "smoking gun" in isolation, protocol assessment flags `[INTERESTED_PARTY, SINGLE_SOURCE]` (incentive to boast to association dues-payers). Three independent finance ministry drafting files, legislative committee timestamps, and central bank costing memos show the decree was drafted four months prior to the association's letter by fiscal technocrats responding to a balance-of-payments deficit (`UNRESOLVED_CONTRADICTION` / falsified timing).
3. **Illustration 3 (Process Tracing Without Van Evera Labels — 1990s Central European Bank Privatization):**
   - *Execution:* Evaluates `ANTECEDENT` (bad-loan overhang) $\to$ `MECHANISM` (EU accession regulatory harmonization vs. domestic fiscal crisis) $\to$ `INTERMEDIATE PROCESS` (inter-ministerial privatization agency negotiations) $\to$ `ACTOR RESPONSE` (state bank recapitalization and foreign strategic sale) $\to$ `CONSEQUENCE` (foreign-owned banking sector) using direct qualitative diagnosticity (`HIGH_DISCRIMINATION` / `NON_DISCRIMINATING`) with zero Van Evera terminology. Fully compliant.
