# Epistemic Integrity in Public Scholarship

Public scholarship translates research; it does not invent facts or exaggerate findings. To maintain scholarly integrity, public writing must adhere to strict epistemic safeguards.

---

## 1. Respecting Claim Ceilings

Every public claim must be warranted by the underlying research:
1. **Correlation is Not Causation:** An econometric association, Toda-Yamamoto lag precedence, or descriptive correlation cannot be publicly advertised as "definitive causal proof." Use calibrated language: *"los datos muestran una fuerte correlación temporal"*, *"el análisis sugiere una relación estrecha"*, rather than *"la econometría demuestra de forma concluyente que X causó Y"*.
2. **Empirical Non-Findings Remain Non-Findings:** If an empirical analysis fails to reject a null hypothesis, fails to find cointegration, or detects statistical insignificance, the public piece must NOT claim that the theoretical prior was confirmed. Never write: *"aunque las pruebas de cointegración fallaron, sabemos que el mecanismo operó con fuerza"*.
3. **Preserve Scope Conditions:** If an economic model or empirical result is derived under specific assumptions (e.g., fixed exchange rates, high idle capacity, price controls), do not present the conclusion as an unconditional universal law.

---

## 2. Evidence Traceability and Data Ethics

- **Traceable Numbers:** Every percentage, dollar amount, index level, or growth figure cited in a public essay must be traceable to verified source data.
- **Autonomous Figures:** Exhibits in public scholarship must be fully autonomous: clear titles stating the main finding, complete axis labels, date markers, and source notes at the bottom.
- **Quotation Integrity:** Distinguish between original author claims and quotes from historical figures or opponents. A quoted reactionary statement must be analyzed critically, not merged into the authorial voice.

---

## 3. Acknowledging Capability Limits

- **Micro Causal Inference:** If a public writing project requires identifying causal impacts via difference-in-differences, instrumental variables, regression discontinuity, or synthetic controls, the system must acknowledge that this capability (`causal_inference_microeconometrics`) currently has **NO CURRENT CANONICAL OWNER** in ARS.
- Do not misroute causal identification requests to applied econometrics (which owns time-series methods only) or pretend that qualitative descriptions substitute for formal identification designs.
