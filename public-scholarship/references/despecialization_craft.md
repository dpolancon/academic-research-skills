# Des-specialization Craft Guide: Academic Research to Public Prose

This reference provides authorial and editorial guidelines for translating specialized academic political economy and economics research into lucid, engaging, and disciplined public writing.

---

## 1. The Principle of Des-specialization

Des-specialization is the intellectual translation of technical models, econometric estimates, and historical arguments into language understandable by non-specialist citizens, workers, and organizers.

It is governed by two complementary rules:
1. **Remove Academic Insulation:** Eliminate insider disciplinary jargon, equation mechanics, and citation-dense debates.
2. **Prevent Vulgarization:** Retain the analytical core, institutional mechanics, and material stakes. Never substitute an emotional slogan, conspiracy theory, or empty moral condemnation for a clear causal explanation.

---

## 2. Technical Vocabulary Translation Matrix

The table below provides illustrative translations across political economy and macroeconomics. **These are contextual translation heuristics, not rigid one-to-one synonyms.** Always adapt the wording to reflect the exact argument and qualifications of the source paper:

| Technical Academic Term | ❌ Misleading / Panfletary Slogan | ✅ Des-specialized Material Formulation | Underlying Mechanism to Preserve |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Balance-of-payments constraint** | *"El bloqueo imperialista que asfixia al pueblo"* | *"La escasez estructural de divisas para importar bienes esenciales"* | Explain that growth requires imported machinery, energy, or medicine, which must be paid for in foreign currency earned through exports or external loans. |
| **Endogenous money** | *"La conspiración de los bancos privados"* | *"La creación de dinero a través del crédito bancario comercial"* | Explain that commercial banks create deposits when they make loans, while central banks supply reserves to settle payments and manage the interest rate. |
| **Sellers' inflation / Markup pricing** | *"La codicia desenfrenada de los empresarios"* | *"La traslación de costos a precios finales para proteger márgenes de beneficio"* | Explain that concentrated firms exploit systemic cost shocks (energy, imports) to maintain or expand their profit margins, generating inflation. |
| **Capital flight / Investment strike** | *"El boicot de los traidores a la patria"* | *"La huelga de inversiones y salida de capitales frente a la incertidumbre distributiva"* | Explain that business owners withhold investment or transfer funds abroad when political changes threaten profit expectations or asset security. |
| **Econometric event study** | *"La prueba matemática infalible"* | *"El seguimiento estadístico diario de la reacción de los mercados financieros"* | Explain how asset prices or exchange rates reacted immediately following a specific policy announcement or political shock. |
| **Unit root / Non-stationarity** | *"El desorden estructural de la serie"* | *"Variables económicas cuyos shocks tienen efectos permanentes en el tiempo"* | Explain that temporary economic shocks permanently shift the trajectory of the series rather than reverting to an old trend. |
| **Fiscal dominance / Monetization** | *"La trampa de imprimir billetes sin control"* | *"El financiamiento directo del gasto público mediante emisión del Banco Central"* | Explain the balance-sheet connection between government debt issuance and central bank monetary accommodation. |

## 3. Structural Mechanics vs. Moralizing Rhetoric

- **Moralizing Adjectives Weakly Persuade:** Describing an economic actor as *"rapaz"*, *"siniestro"*, *"parasitario"*, or *"salvaje"* signals emotional outrage but explains nothing about how the economy functions.
- **Forensic Institutional Exposition Convinces:** Persuasion rests on demonstrating concrete material mechanisms:
  - Who owns the productive asset?
  - What share of the market is controlled by the top three firms?
  - How did regulatory changes allow wholesale cost increases to be passed onto consumer bills?
  - What interest rate did international creditors demand to roll over sovereign debt?

When an author draft relies on moral adjectives, the editor should identify the underlying economic mechanism and rewrite the sentence to showcase the material fact.

---

## 4. Paragraph Dynamics and Rhythm

- **Sentence Length:** Alternate between analytical sentences explaining the mechanism (18–25 words) and short, punchy declarative conclusions (8–12 words).
- **Paragraph Bounds:** Standard paragraphs should run 3 to 5 sentences (~60–90 words). Avoid dense academic "wall of text" blocks that run 15 lines without a break.
- **The Dry Punchline:** Conclude complex explanatory paragraphs with a decisive sentence that anchors the political-economic takeaway.
