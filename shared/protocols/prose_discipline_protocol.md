## 1. Purpose

The Academic Research Skills (ARS) Prose Discipline Protocol establishes a cross-domain editorial standard for identifying and repairing mechanical rhetoric, stylistic clutter, metadata leakage, and syntactic monotony in scholarly manuscripts.

Its primary objective is to cultivate clear, rigorous, and direct academic prose across all disciplines without flattening theoretical vocabulary, erasing disciplinary nuances, or compromising technical precision.

---

## 2. Ownership Boundary

The Prose Discipline Protocol is strictly classified as a **`CROSS_DOMAIN_SHARED_PROTOCOL`**:

1. **Protocol, Not Skill:** It is not an independent writing skill, an autonomous drafting agent, or a competing review engine.
2. **Consultative Standard:** It defines evaluation criteria, diagnostic pattern families, and editorial guidelines that canonical skills consume during drafting, self-evaluation, and revision.
3. **No Capability Monopoly Infringement:** It does not own the manuscript lifecycle, economics disciplinary standards, or peer-review adjudication.
4. **Offline and Rule-Based:** Its standards are accessible to both human editors and deterministic verification scripts without requiring runtime model inference.

---

## 3. Non-Goals

The protocol explicitly disclaims the following objectives:

- **Not an AI Detector:** The protocol does not detect, score, or infer whether text was generated by a large language model or authored by a human.
- **Not a Style Flattener:** It does not enforce uniform sentence lengths, ban complex syntax, or discourage legitimate scholarly abstraction.
- **Not a Disciplinary Dictator:** It does not prescribe empirical methodology, model formulation, or theoretical validity.
- **Not an Automatic Rewriter:** It provides diagnostic signals and editorial principles; it never executes blind, global search-and-replace transformations.

## 7. Category A / B / C Terminology Protection Model

To prevent automated tools or well-intentioned editors from stripping necessary theoretical precision, all vocabulary under review must be classified into three distinct categories:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ CATEGORY A: Protected Technical & Conceptual Vocabulary        │
│ Action: RETAIN INTACT. Strict immunity from deletion/editing.    │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ CATEGORY B: Context-Dependent Disciplinary Terminology          │
│ Action: REVIEW IN CONTEXT. Simplify only when clustered or      │
│         gratuitous, preserving exact technical meaning.          │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ CATEGORY C: Replaceable Scaffolding & Inflated Pastiche         │
│ Action: REWRITE / ELIMINATE. Delete vacuous intensifiers and    │
│         replace branded metaphors with direct empirical terms.  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Category A: Protected Technical & Conceptual Vocabulary (RETAIN)
Terms that name a legitimate theoretical, empirical, mathematical, institutional, or historical concept. Replacing or removing these terms destroys substantive analytical meaning.
- *Universal / Cross-Domain Examples:* `eigenvalues`, `null hypothesis`, `orthogonal projection`, `asymptotic normality`, `counterfactual variance`, `heteroskedasticity`.
- *Heterodox & Political Economy Examples:* `rate of exploitation`, `transformation elasticity`, `balance-of-payments constraint`, `companion matrix eigenvalues`, `Sraffa-Kurz choice of technique`, `reserve army of labor`, `Johansen trace test`, `supermultiplier`, `effective demand`, `organic composition of capital`.
- *Invariant Rule:* Category A terms are **immune from automated deletion or replacement**. Flagging a Category A term as "jargon" is an error.

### Category B: Context-Dependent Disciplinary Phrasing (REVIEW)
Defensible academic language that is conceptually sound but becomes an editorial distraction through excessive local repetition, clustering, or rote imitation.
- *Examples:* `under an unbalanced growth closure`, `institutional framework`, `transmission mechanism`, `dynamics of accumulation`, `structural heterogeneity`.
- *Invariant Rule:* Category B terms are evaluated in context. Edit only when clustered conspicuously within adjacent paragraphs, and only if disciplinary precision is fully preserved.

### Category C: Replaceable Scaffolding & Generic Rhetorical Tells (REWRITE DIRECTLY)
Language that adds zero analytical or empirical content, existing solely to organize, narrate, intensify, or brand ordinary authorial steps.
- *Generic Intensifiers:* `crucially`, `decisive`, `profound`, `deep`, `stark`, `striking`, `central`, `remarkable`, `systematically`.
- *Branded Metaphors:* `empirical tournament`, `admissibility gates`, `black-box screening protocol`, `deliberately stress test`, `profound institutional divergence`.
- *Workflow & Metadata Leaks:* `authorization gate`, `retained criteria`, `editing ledger`, `interpretive move`, `analytical move`, `selection filter`.
- *Invariant Rule:* Category C phrasing is presumptively editable. Replace with direct subject-matter description or delete entirely.

## 8. Claim-Strength Discipline

Academic writing requires precise calibration between the strength of an assertion and the underlying empirical research design. Claim verbs must strictly track evidentiary foundations:

| Research Design / Evidentiary Foundation | Calibrated Permissible Verbs | Forbidden Over-Claims |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Descriptive / Stylized Trends** | `documents`, `records`, `shows`, `traces`, `is consistent with`, `indicates` | `proves`, `demonstrates conclusively`, `confirms truth of` |
| **Granger Causality / VAR / Predictive** | `predicts`, `precedes conditionally`, `contains incremental predictive information`, `forecasts` | `causes`, `drives directly`, `forces` |
| **Cointegration / Long-Run Relations** | `identifies a long-run relation`, `rejects the zero-rank null hypothesis`, `estimates the cointegrating vector` | `validates the true production law`, `proves the model` |
| **Impulse Responses (IRF / GIRF)** | `conditional dynamic response`, `exhibits persistent response over reported horizons`, `decays toward baseline` | `mechanically dictates`, `proves permanent causality` |
| **Quasi-Experimental / Identification** | `identifies the local average effect`, `estimates conditional effect under parallel trends` | `proves universal law`, `eradicates alternative mechanisms` |

> **Core Principle:** Assertive verbs must be earned by empirical design. Point estimates, standard errors, and confidence bounds convey strength; loaded rhetorical verbs do not.

## 9. Prose-Pattern Families

The protocol formalizes **11 Auditing Pattern Families** as diagnostic categories for editorial review:

### Family 1: Syntactic Monotony & Uniform Cadence
- *Pattern:* Successive sentences adhering to identical grammatical structures (e.g., three consecutive Subject-Verb-Object sentences of 22–26 words).
- *Guidance:* Interlock short declarative sentences (6–12 words) with compound, analytically nuanced sentences (25–35 words) to create dynamic reading cadence.

### Family 2: Repetitive Sentence Openings
- *Pattern:* Three or more consecutive sentences beginning with the same word, pronoun, or participial clause (e.g., `This...`, `This...`, `This...` or `By analyzing...`, `By constructing...`).
- *Guidance:* Vary grammatical openings: lead with substantive empirical subjects, conditional clauses, or prepositional phrases.

### Family 3: Excessive Signposting & Metanarrative
- *Pattern:* Throat-clearing discourse where the author narrates their own analytical process instead of discussing the object of study (`taken together`, `in this sense`, `in this context`, `what matters here is`, `the key point is`, `this reveals that`).
- *Guidance:* Delete the signpost; begin the sentence directly with the substantive empirical claim.

### Family 4: Canned Transitions & Mechanical Connectors
- *Pattern:* Rote, formulaic paragraph-opening adverbs (`Furthermore,`, `Moreover,`, `Additionally,`, `In addition,`).
- *Guidance:* Establish logical transitions through thematic continuity and evidential progression rather than mechanical glue words.

### Family 5: Repetitive Contrast Formulas
- *Pattern:* Overuse of binary antithesis templates (`X, not Y`; `not X but Y`; `rather than X, Y`; `not merely X, it also Y`; `far from X, Y`).
- *Guidance:* Use contrast formulas only when distinguishing genuinely competing theoretical models or conflicting hypotheses; convert secondary contrasts to direct declarative assertions.

### Family 6: Abstraction Stacking & Theoretical Density
- *Pattern:* Piling abstract conceptual categories without intermediate empirical grounding or numerical anchors.
- *Guidance:* Anchor abstract theoretical formulations with stylized facts, concrete parameter magnitudes, or descriptive data comparisons within the same paragraph.

### Family 7: Nominalization Clusters
- *Pattern:* Sentences clogged with chains of abstract nouns ending in *-tion*, *-ment*, or *-ance* (`the operationalization of the conceptualization of the institutionalization...`).
- *Guidance:* Convert at least one nominalization per sentence into an active, concrete verb.

### Family 8: Evaluative Adjective & Adverb Loading
- *Pattern:* Subjective intensifying modifiers attempting to substitute for statistical significance (`decisive`, `profound`, `stark`, `striking`, `dramatic`, `crucial`, `precisely`).
- *Guidance:* Eliminate the modifier; allow test statistics, confidence intervals, and point estimates to convey analytical importance.

### Family 9: Rhetorical Parallelism & Clustered Triads
- *Pattern:* Repeated three-part noun, verb, or adjective lists, particularly after colons or in section summaries (`rigorous, transparent, and robust`).
- *Guidance:* Retain triads only when all three terms designate distinct, essential operational categories; collapse or vary syntax otherwise.

### Family 10: Repetitive Paragraph Narrative Templates
- *Pattern:* Formulaic paragraph staging: Literature claim $\to$ Sudden adversarial pivot (`Yet...`) $\to$ Author's corrective claim $\to$ Grand concluding aphorism.
- *Guidance:* Allow paragraphs to conclude quietly with evidence, data summaries, or empirical bounds rather than manufactured dramatic peaks.

### Family 11: Production Metadata & Scaffolding Leakage
- *Pattern:* Internal workflow artifacts, script paths, LaTeX build labels, or agent prompts leaking into visible draft prose (`\texttt{output/report_...}`, `admissibility gates`, `retained criteria`, `editing ledger`).
- *Guidance:* Replace with standard scholarly references or methodological descriptions of the empirical system under study.

## 10. Human Judgment Requirements

Prose evaluation is fundamentally an interpretive editorial activity:

1. **Context Determines Quality:** A sentence structure that is tedious in an introductory overview may be essential in a mathematical derivation.
2. **Authorial Voice:** Distinctive scholar cadences and legitimate rhetorical choices must be respected.
3. **No Blind Automated Overwrites:** Diagnostic tools must emit review signals, never automated blind replacements. Human inspection remains the final authority.

---

## 11. Deterministic vs. Judgmental Checks

The protocol establishes a strict dividing line between what can be checked mechanically and what requires human scholarly judgment:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ DETERMINISTIC CHECKS (Tool-Enforced via Regex/AST)          │
│ • Exact Category C scaffolding pattern matches              │
│ • Frequency counts of canned transition adverbs             │
│ • Consecutive sentence-start repetitions (N >= 3)           │
│ • Consecutive contrast template repetitions                 │
│ • Metadata/script path pattern leaks (e.g. \texttt{output}) │
│ • Category A protection invariants (never flag as jargon)    │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ JUDGMENTAL REVIEWS (Protocol-Guided Human/Agent Inspection) │
│ • Appropriateness of theoretical abstraction                │
│ • Elegance and cadence of argumentative prose               │
│ • Scientific validity of claim verbs against methodology    │
│ • Disciplinary register appropriateness                     │
│ • Resolution of complex Category B clustered terminology    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 12. False-Positive Policy

1. **Conservative Flagging:** A tool should flag a pattern only when it exceeds clear mechanical thresholds (e.g., 3+ identical sentence starts, 2+ contrast templates in a single paragraph).
2. **Warning $\ne$ Error:** Flags are classified as `INFO` or `REVIEW`. A flag indicates that a human editor should inspect the passage, not that the prose is grammatically or scholarly invalid.
3. **Domain Exemption:** When technical prose is processed, recognized Category A domain terms must be explicitly suppressed from jargon alerts.

---

## 13. Technical Vocabulary Protection

To enforce the invariant that **technical language is not guilty by association**:

- Under no circumstances shall an automated prose tool recommend simplifying or replacing recognized mathematical, econometrical, or theoretical terms (e.g., `eigenvalues`, `Leontief`, `supermultiplier`, `cointegrating rank`).
- Any diagnostic finding that suggests replacing a Category A term with colloquial vocabulary must be treated as a tool bug.

---

## 14. Revision Recommendations

When revising flagged text, editors should adhere to four core techniques:

1. **Direct Empirical Naming:** Write about the *phenomenon*, not the *editing apparatus*. (Say *"The cointegration vector is the relationship under test,"* not *"The empirical object is the cointegration vector."*)
2. **De-intensification:** Delete unearned adjectives (`profound`, `decisive`, `striking`) and let point estimates and standard errors establish empirical weight.
3. **Declarative Restructuring:** Convert formulaic `not X, but Y` structures into direct positive assertions.
4. **Cadence Alternation:** Break up uniform sentence lengths by combining related clauses or introducing concise declarative summaries.

---

## 15. AI-Authorship Non-Inference Rule

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL BINDING INVARIANT:**
> **Under no circumstances may this protocol, or any tool implementing it, claim to detect AI authorship, calculate machine-generation probabilities, or classify text as human vs. synthetic.**

- Flagged prose patterns represent common stylistic habits, rhetorical mannerisms, and drafting scaffolding that occur in both human drafts and model outputs.
- A flagged pattern is **never evidence of AI authorship**.
- Terms like "AI tell", "machine cadence", or "hallucination score" are strictly prohibited from diagnostic output schemas. All findings are classified purely as stylistic and editorial patterns.
