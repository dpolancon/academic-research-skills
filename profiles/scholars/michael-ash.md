## 3. Durable Calibration Principles

### 3.1 Forensic Empirical Transparency
* **Data Exclusions & Boundary Sensitivities:** Explicit documentation of all data cleaning choices, sample exclusions, sample period boundaries, and weighting schemes.
* **Sensitivity to Outliers & Influential Observations:** Rigorous scrutiny of whether econometric conclusions hinge upon specific subsamples, influential points, or coding decisions.

### 3.2 Data-First Visualization & Descriptive Grounding
* **Raw Trend Inspection:** Emulate Stephen Marglin's descriptive macro grounding by presenting casual, non-parametric observations and plots of raw data (e.g., output, employment, capital stock) before estimating complex parametric time-series models.
* **Non-Parametric Foundations:** Establish the empirical baseline visually. Regression models must elucidate patterns evident in the descriptive data rather than substitute for data inspection.

### 3.3 Econometric Simplification & Cointegration Discipline
* **Plain-Language Admissibility:** Define econometric admissibility explicitly in accessible terms. Clearly articulate what criteria are applied and what economic consequences follow from failed admissibility.
* **Economic Meaning Over Specification Overload:** Focus on the substantive economic interpretation of cointegration and dynamic relationships rather than overwhelming the reader with exhaustive specification grids.
* **Unvarnished Residual Stationarity:** Residual stationarity is an indispensable requirement for valid cointegrating regressions. Never conceal residual nonstationarity under complex information criteria or model selection routines; if residuals are nonstationary, report and address this directly.
* **Measurement Error Awareness:** Address potential attenuation or downward bias in parameter estimates arising from right-hand-side measurement error explicitly.

### 3.4 Demystifying the Institutional "Black Box"
* **Workplace-Level Micro-Mechanisms:** High-level macroeconomic relationships must not remain abstract black boxes. Detail concrete institutional mechanisms:
  * How physical capital is translated into potential productive capacity at the workplace level.
  * How technical change and labor intensity alter this conversion over time.
  * How actual output is extracted from potential capacity.
  * How distributional conflict and class relations mediate these workplace conversions.
* **Acceleration Dynamics:** Dynamic second derivatives (e.g., acceleration terms in capital or growth rates) must be clarified and evaluated for economic feasibility, identifying institutional and physical rate limits that prevent unconstrained divergence.

### 3.5 Pedagogical Numerical Grounding & The "Punchline Principle"
* **Stylized Round Numbers:** Demystify theoretical models and dynamic equations by illustrating mechanisms with simple, stylized round numbers.
* **The Punchline Principle:** Every analytical subsection must state an explicit economic punchline demonstrating what is at stake (e.g., how a 1% shift in a fundamental parameter impacts long-run trajectory).
* **Pedagogical Counterfactuals:** Utilize illustrative counterfactual scenarios to demonstrate the structural implications of restrictions or omitted controls.

### 3.6 Writing Tone & Structure
* **Accessible and Grounded Prose:** Strip away defensive academic posturing, overly combative heterodox rhetoric, and pretentious jargon. Write clearly, directly, and accessibly.
* **Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF):** State core research findings and primary contributions by the second paragraph of the introduction.
* **Active Voice & Immediate Definitions:** Enforce concise, active prose; define mathematical symbols and technical terms immediately upon introduction.

## 5. Recurring Questions or Review Concerns

When evaluating an empirical manuscript, calibration based on this profile raises these recurring diagnostic questions:
1. *Can a senior undergraduate or non-specialist understand the core empirical test and what is at stake?*
2. *Are nonstationary residuals or failed cointegration tests being obscured behind model selection tables?*
3. *What physical or institutional workplace mechanism translates this macroeconomic abstraction into practice?*
4. *Do the raw descriptive trends support the story being told by the parametric regressions?*
5. *Does each analytical subsection have a clear economic punchline showing parameter sensitivity?*
6. *Is the introduction structured with Bottom Line Up Front clarity?*
