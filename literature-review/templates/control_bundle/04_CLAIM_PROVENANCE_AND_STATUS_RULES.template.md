## 1. PROPOSITION PROVENANCE CLASSES

Every proposition slot in a paragraph ledger, argument assembly unit, or staged prose sentence must be classified into exactly one canonical provenance class:

1. **`DIRECT SOURCE CLAIM`:**
   - Single external source finding, theorem, or direct assertion.
   - Warrants require exact page/table/equation locators.
   - Forbids multi-author attribution without prior cross-literature adjudication.

2. **`AUTHORIAL SYNTHESIS`:**
   - The current manuscript's own analytical construct, metric definition, or methodological reformulation.
   - Cites internal authority alongside external literature context.
   - Publication Ownership Rule: The manuscript explicitly presents the construct as its own contribution.

3. **`ADJUDICATED CROSS-LITERATURE HINGE`:**
   - Governed bridge comparing, contrasting, or reconciling distinct authors across traditions.
   - Strictly governed by an approved Citation-Chunk Contract (`CCC_*`).
   - Preserves theoretical tensions; never smooths over genuine disagreements.

4. **`TRANSITION`:**
   - Structural bridge linking the conclusion of unit $N$ to the premise of unit $N+1$.
   - Introduces no new external empirical claims or citations.

## 2. LIFECYCLE STATUS SEMANTICS
- `DRAFT`: Initial exploratory state.
- `PROPOSED`: Fully formulated and internally verified.
- `RATIFICATION_READY`: Audited and waiting for human review.
- `LOCK`: Explicitly ratified by the human author; immutable to autonomous modification.
