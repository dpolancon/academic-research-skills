## 1. SOVEREIGN PERIMETER PRINCIPLE
**THE HUMAN AUTHOR APPROVES CHANGES TO THE EPISTEMIC PERIMETER, NOT EVERY MECHANICAL TRANSFORMATION WITHIN IT.**

---

## 2. EXCLUSIVE HUMAN RESERVED POWERS
1. **Corpus Admission:** Admitting new external sources into the active corpus.
2. **Architecture Change:** Modifying the canonical sequence, scope, or number of units.
3. **Scholarly Lock Ratification:** Transitioning any contract, ledger, assembly, or prose unit to `LOCK`.
4. **Contribution Hierarchy:** Adjudicating primary vs secondary contributions.
5. **Novelty & Priority:** Authorizing claims of priority ("first", "unique").
6. **Manuscript Prose Acceptance:** Authorizing prose insertion into paper draft sections.
7. **Git Commit / Push:** Authorizing version control mutations.

---

## 3. MANDATORY ESCALATION TRIGGERS
Autonomous loops must immediately halt and escalate to human review if:
1. An uncataloged external source is required;
2. Bibliographic identity or pagination cannot be verified;
3. Saturated source evidence remains insufficient after 4 repair iterations;
4. Proposition contradicts executable code/data repository;
5. Discrepancy arises between internal methodology and external sources;
6. A proposed change would alter or weaken an existing human `LOCK`;
7. Corpus expansion or architecture modification is proposed;
8. Contribution hierarchy or novelty claim adjudication is required;
9. An autonomous repair loop fails to converge within 4 cycles (or shows zero progress across 2);
10. An unledgered claim is required during prose drafting.
